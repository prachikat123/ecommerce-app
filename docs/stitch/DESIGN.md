## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-trust, progressive, and technologically empowered agricultural commerce ecosystem. It blends modern corporate clarity with tactile, grounded agricultural reliability. The interface balances technical credibility (for input certifications, government subsidies, and seed purity guarantees) with effortless consumer ease, catering to progressive farmers, agri-dealers, and institutional buyers across Tier 1 through Tier 4 India.

The visual style is **Modern Tactile & High-Clarity**: crisp white structural surfaces suspended over pale agrarian neutrals, accented by organic verdant tones and radiant harvest amber. Visual clutter is stripped away in favor of generous whitespace, immediate transactional readability, high-contrast numeric price hierarchies, and robust, thumb-friendly hit targets optimized for outdoor, one-handed mobile usage.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional balance of vitality, authority, and transactional confidence:

- **Primary Forest Green (`#15803d`, with `#166534` for deep surfaces and high-contrast text states)**: Represents lush crop health, institutional trust, and certified quality. Used for core brand elements, primary CTAs, active navigation items, and verification seals.
- **Secondary Leaf Green (`#22c55e`)**: High-energy vegetative green, utilized for promotional highlights, micro-interactions, stock availability badges, and success affirmations.
- **Harvest Amber (`#d97706`, grounding to `#b45309`)**: A warm, sun-ripened crop accent used to spotlight critical financial details: high-yield discounts, flash deals, seasonal offers, and direct-to-farm subsidies.
- **Neutral Slate (`#0f172a` to `#475569`)**: Dark slate provides superior readability under varying natural sunlight compared to pure black, minimizing eye fatigue.
- **Surface Canvas (`#f8fafc` blending to light tinted `#f0fdf4`)**: Evokes cleanliness and open air, providing soft tonal contrast against pure `#ffffff` product cards.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for expressive, friendly headlines and pricing displays with **Inter** for dense, multi-tiered tabular data, transactional labels, and micro-copy. 

Special emphasis is placed on numerical rendering: the Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) is treated with tabular alignment, weighted equally to the primary amount, and paired directly with unit qualifiers (e.g., `/ quintal`, `/ 50 kg bag`) in secondary body styling to prevent ambiguous pricing interpretations.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible 12-column grid system for desktop and a 4-column responsive fluid grid for mobile viewports, bounded by a maximum container width of `1280px`. 

Because mobile field conditions demand frictionless tapability, interactive controls enforce a minimum physical touch target of `48px` (expanding to `52px` on bottom-anchored actions). Mobile horizontal page margins are anchored at `1rem` (`16px`) to maximize horizontal screen real estate for product grid density, while card-internal padding maintains a strict `space-lg` (`16px`) rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a refined layer hierarchy combining soft chromatic shadows and low-contrast borders:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Light foundational background (`#f8fafc`) mixed with subtle radial gradients of `#f0fdf4`.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Surfaces)**: Pure `#ffffff` surface, bounded by a 1px solid hairline border in `#e2e8f0` and an ambient, plant-tinted shadow (`0 2px 6px -1px rgba(22, 101, 52, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`).
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Product Tiles)**: Raised depth highlighting direct engagement. Border shifts to `#bbf7d0`, shadow expands to `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(22, 101, 52, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Bar & Bottom Sheets)**: Used for checkout bars, language pickers, and filter modals. Deep shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard:
- Cards, modal containers, and hero banners feature a smooth `16px` (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`) corner radius.
- Buttons, input fields, quantity incrementers, and select chips feature `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius for sharp structural integrity.
- Verification pills, certification tags, and discount stickers take a full pill treatment (`9999px`) to create distinction from actionable structural buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Add to Cart", "Buy Now")**: Solid `#15803d` background, `#ffffff` text, minimum height `48px`, font weight 600. Includes integrated subtle tactile active compression (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Bulk Quotation", "View Details")**: Crisp `#ffffff` background with 1.5px border in `#15803d`, text in `#15803d`.
- **Harvest Accent CTA (e.g., "Claim Subsidy", "Flash Offer")**: Solid `#d97706` background with `#ffffff` text.

### Cards (Product, Crop Category & Fertilizer)
- **Product Tiles**: Crisp white (`#ffffff`) background, 16px radius, bordered with `#e2e8f0`. Images are framed with an off-white baseline canvas (`#f8fafc`) to preserve transparent product cutouts.
- **Badges Zone**: Top-left contains stacked badges:
  - *Discount Badge*: Harvest gold `#fef3c7` with `#b45309` bold text.
  - *Authenticity Badge*: Forest green tint `#dcfce7` with `#166534` text and an official ICAR/Govt shield icon.
- **Pack Size Selector Chips**: Inline scrollable segment containing weights (`1 kg`, `5 L`, `50 kg Bag`) with an active state of `#15803d` text over a `#f0fdf4` fill and 1px `#15803d` stroke.
- **Pricing Cluster**: Primary price rendered in `price-card` (`Plus Jakarta Sans Bold`) with immediate strikethrough MRP (`#94a3b8`) and percentage-off callout.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- **Text Inputs**: Height `48px`, 8px radius, `#ffffff` fill, `#cbd5e1` resting border, shifting to a 2px `#15803d` focus ring.
- **Quantity Selector**: Large thumb-accessible Stepper (`-` and `+`) with a minimum width of `44px` per control for simple field updates without requiring keyboard entry.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Category & Crop Season Filters**: 36px height, rounded pill shape, resting background `#f1f5f9` with `#475569` text. Selected state switches to `#15803d` background with `#ffffff` text.

### Specialized Agriculture Components
- **Government / ICAR Verification Tag**: High-trust pill featuring an official emblem silhouette, guaranteed purity mark, and serial authentication link.
- **Mandi Price Comparative Bar**: Compact two-tone micro-bar showing local Mandi rates versus KrishiBazaar direct-to-farm savings.
